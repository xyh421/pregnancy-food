# Weekly menu maintenance guide

This guide helps maintain the public meal-planning website without exposing personal information.

## Content review

- Check each recipe title, ingredients, approximate portions and preparation instructions.
- Make the shopping list consistent with the meals shown for the selected week.
- Confirm that week rotation and mode switching still work on mobile.
- Treat nutrition information as general guidance, not a replacement for individualized clinical advice.
- Check that any external recipe or video link is relevant and still accessible; do not claim a video is the most watched without current verification.

## Food safety

- Thoroughly cook meat, seafood and eggs; avoid unpasteurized dairy products.
- Wash produce and separate raw foods from ready-to-eat foods.
- Refrigerate perishables promptly and follow local food-safety guidance.
- For postpartum meal planning, prioritize varied, practical meals and hydration rather than unverified recovery claims.

## Privacy and release

Do not include family names, medical records, precise locations or private schedules in public pages. Verify GitHub Pages deployment, HTTPS and the custom domain after changes.
